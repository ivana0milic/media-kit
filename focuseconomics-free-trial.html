Why consensus

Last year's best forecaster is rarely this year's

We compared 165,172 pairs of forecaster rankings across more than 20 years of data. Rankings barely carry over from one year to the next, so betting on a single forecaster is a gamble. The consensus takes that risk away.

How we measured this

For every indicator, country and year, each panelist and the Consensus were ranked by how close their forecast came to the actual figure. Across the 165,172 year-to-year ranking comparisons, the average correlation between one year's ranking and the next was just 0.06. In one example, a panelist ranked 1st one year fell to 10th the next, while another rose from 16th to 1st. The Consensus scored better on average than every one of the largest panelists, and landed in the bottom quarter only 3.9% of the time.

FAQ

Questions before you start?

How long does the trial last?

We agree the length with you based on what you need to evaluate. Most trials run 1–2 weeks, and public sector organizations can have up to 1–2 months.

What will I have access to?

Whatever your use case needs: countries, commodities, indicators and add-ons. Some features, like Databank for bulk data export, are usually reserved for subscribers. You can still download the data behind any chart.

Why is there a demo call first?

It's the quickest way to learn the platform, and it lets us set your trial up with the right coverage so you're not stuck working out where everything is.

Why do you need a company email?

Trials are for professional use, and a company email lets us set up access for your organization. Personal addresses (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) aren't accepted.

What happens when the trial ends?

We'll meet to review how it went and whether a subscription makes sense for your team.

How is my data used?

Only to set up and support your trial, in line with our Privacy Policy. We never sell your details.
