What you will take away

How a Consensus is built

The FocusEconomics Consensus Forecast is the average projection from a panel of leading banks and consultancies. Combining many views reduces the error in any single forecast.

What disagreement tells you

The gap between the highest and lowest forecast is a clear measure of uncertainty, showing where economists are confident and where they are not.

The longer-term picture

Beyond the next year or two, aging populations, shrinking labor forces and automation shape the outlook, with forecasts available out to 2035.
