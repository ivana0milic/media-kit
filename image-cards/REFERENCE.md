# Newsletter image card: reference spec

Approved example: `REFERENCE_newsletter_card.png` (Lula / Brazil article, "Forecasts in Focus").

- **Canvas:** 1500 x 785 px (1.91:1), the LinkedIn newsletter cover ratio. The cover is cropped to this shape, so keep everything inside it.
- **Photo:** full-width landscape photo with a pale, clear sky. About 85 px of matching sky is added above it so the title has room. The photo is squashed vertically to fit (about 27%). Small boats or clutter behind the logo are retouched away.
- **Title:** Open Sans ExtraBold (800), 62 px, line-height 1.08, letter-spacing -0.8 px.
  - Navy `#13234B`, with the key phrase in blue `#2F5E8C`.
  - Top-left, 80 px from the left edge and 30 px from the top.
  - Two lines, wrapped after "mean". Must not touch the photo subject.
- **Logo:** white FocusEconomics wordmark, 36 px tall, bottom-right (80 px from the right, 30 px from the bottom), on plain dark area.
- **No overlays:** no gradients or dark shading over the photo.
- **Title copy:** use the article's own title, not invented headlines.
